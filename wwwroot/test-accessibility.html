Keyboard Navigation Instructions

  • Tab: Navigate between language options (English → Tamil)
  • Enter/Space: Activate the focused language option
  • Focus Indicator: Focused button will have a bright golden outline and background highlight

Navigation Flow:

  1. English button is highlighted by default
  2. Press Tab to move focus to Tamil button
  3. Press Enter or Space to switch to Tamil language
  4. Press Tab again to cycle back to English button
